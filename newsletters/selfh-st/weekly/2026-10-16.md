## Weekly Highlights

- **Bitwarden** [switched to a dual-licensing model](https://community.bitwarden.com/t/published-version-update-in-app-stores/102750?ref=selfh.st), and while they claim users won't be impacted, I find their "All ***current*** features are available in both versions" statement (emphasis mine) a bit concerning
- The **Endurain** project made the jump [back to GitHub](https://github.com/endurain-project/endurain?ref=selfh.st) after migrating to Codeberg just a few months ago (perfectly reasonable explanation from the dev [here](https://docs.endurain.com/blog/2026/10/09/feature-freeze-update-4/?ref=selfh.st))
- **GitLab** is warning users to upgrade after researchers discovered a vulnerability that [allows unauthenticated users to execute code under specific conditions](https://docs.gitlab.com/releases/patches/patch-release-gitlab-19-4-2-released/?ref=selfh.st#cve-2026-97229---integer-overflow-issue-in-cicd-regular-expression-compiler-impacts-gitlab-ceee)
- Despite [last week's warnings](https://selfh.st/weekly/2026-10-09/), the **LinuxServer.io** team [added containers for the Artcraft suite](https://info.linuxserver.io/issues/2026-10-15-craftapps/?ref=selfh.st) of Adobe alternatives (clearly they don't read this newsletter)
- **Sonarr v5 **is [officially in beta](https://www.reddit.com/r/sonarr/comments/1x4cplm/sonarr_v5_now_in_beta/?ref=selfh.st) and features an updated interface that doesn't look like it was ripped straight from the 90s
- A Redditor [created a neat hardware solution](https://www.reddit.com/r/homeassistant/comments/1x47pi5/keybridge_20_is_finally_here/?ref=selfh.st) that allows users to control **Home Assistant** automations from USB keyboards and devices (and no, I don't care if you can build a cheaper version yourself)
- The latest **UniFi Network** update [picked up support for Matter and Thread](https://blog.ui.com/article/introducing-unifi-network-11?ref=selfh.st)
- **Google** announced they're giving users a private space in Google Play to [upload their vibe coded Android apps](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/google-play-updates-october-2026/?ref=selfh.st)
- **Apple **[announced their own* lineup of smart home devices](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/introducing-apple-home-collection-a-new-lineup-of-smart-home-accessories/?ref=selfh.st) to be launched in 2027 (*created by LG and Schneider)
- Multiple people are developing PS2 **Jellyfin** clients for some reason ([1](https://github.com/danielnuld/orbit-jellyfin?ref=selfh.st), [2](https://github.com/jimmyBizMobile/jellyfin-ps2?ref=selfh.st))
- I found my new favorite **satire site** – [Open is the New Closed](https://openfatigue.ai/?ref=selfh.st)

## Content Spotlight

Meet **Homelable**, a self-hosted homelab infrastructure visualization and monitoring platform. With Homelable, users can easily keep tabs on deployed services and devices via scheduled network scans, device maps, and live statuses. Features include built-in documentation, a visual rack builder, Zigbee and Z-Wave mapping, health checks, and a Home Assistant integration.

**Homelable** can be [easily deployed via Docker or bare metal](https://github.com/Pouzor/homelable/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md?ref=selfh.st), with varying levels of complexity based on the desired functionality.

## Command Line Corner

Preface a filename with **>** to clear the contents of the file directly from the command line:

```bash
$ cat example.txt
  Self-Host Weekly
$ > example.txt
$ cat example.txt
$
```
