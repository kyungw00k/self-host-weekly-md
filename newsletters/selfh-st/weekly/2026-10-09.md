## Weekly Highlights

A [new suite of open source Adobe alternatives](https://github.com/storytold?ref=selfh.st) stirred up some controversy this week after being called out as rip-offs of existing projects – most notably the community favorite [Photopea](https://www.photopea.com/?ref=selfh.st), whose developer was [among the first to raise the alarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20261006025310/https://github.com/eolix/photosuite/issues/77).

To make matters more confusing, the two projects at the center of the conversation – *PhotoSuite* and *PhotoCraft* – appear to be connected. The alleged order of events is difficult to follow, but can be pieced together thanks to some helpful Reddit and Hacker News comment threads ([1](https://www.reddit.com/r/selfhosted/comments/1x0eu16/selfhosted_photoshop_and_acrobat_pro/pek8kix/?ref=selfh.st), [2](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49972730&ref=selfh.st)):

- [PhotoSuite](https://www.photosuite.app/?ref=selfh.st), a near-clone of Photopea, is released
- The developer of Photopea [calls the project out](https://web.archive.org/web/20261006025310/https://github.com/eolix/photosuite/issues/77) and [reports it to GitHub](https://x.com/photopeacom/status/2106877355738140875?ref=selfh.st), after which the repo is temporarily closed and scrubbed of the accusation
- [PhotoCraft](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft?ref=selfh.st) is subsequently released
- The PhotoSuite repo [re-opens](https://github.com/eolix/photosuite?ref=selfh.st), crediting PhotoCraft as the app's "foundation"
- The new claim is immediately scrutinized given the timestamps from PhotoSuite's repo pre-date PhotoCraft's existence

For now, I'd recommend steering clear of the new alternatives until they can prove they're more than just vibe coded derivatives of somebody else's hard work.

(This isn't Photopea's first run-in with the self-hosting crowd, either – a few years ago, the *Noted.lol* editor [publicly scoffed at its self-hosted pricing](https://web.archive.org/web/20220730224722/https://github.com/photopea/photopea/issues/4870). The developer just can't catch a break...)

In other news and activity:

- **Nabu Casa** (Home Assistant) [is renaming their cloud offering to 'Link'](https://www.nabucasa.com/news/2026-10-02-cloud-becomes-link/?ref=selfh.st) given the modern negative connotation with cloud services
- **Hemmelig**, an encrypted secrets sharing platform, [has been archived on GitHub](https://github.com/HemmeligOrg/Hemmelig.app?ref=selfh.st)
- **Let's Encrypt** [announced they're shortening the lifetime of free SSL certificates](https://letsencrypt.org/2026/10/07/64-day-certs?ref=selfh.st) from 90 to 64 days in February 2027
- **Ilograph**, an online interactive diagram service, published [this neat visualization of Immich's upload flow](https://app.ilograph.com/demo.ilograph.Immich/Upload%2520Asset?ref=selfh.st)
- The **NVIDIA Shield TV**, a 7 year-old media streaming device popular with the homelab crowd, received a [$100 price increase](https://www.bestbuy.com/product/nvidia-shield-android-tv-pro-16gb-4k-hdr-streaming-device-with-google-assistant-and-geforce-now-black/J3L2TZ9S46/sku/6370425?ref=selfh.st) due to AI-driven hardware inflation
- **Pop!_OS** is the latest in the line of projects to [ban AI-generated contributions](https://github.com/pop-os/pop/blame/master/CONTRIBUTING.md?ref=selfh.st)
- **Anthropic** updated their privacy policy and [wants you to be nicer to Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-usage-policy-update?ref=selfh.st) (see the "Addressing abusive behavior toward our models*"* section)
- Yes, but [can it play Quake](https://github.com/BenMcLean/CloudyQuake?ref=selfh.st)?

## Content Spotlight

Meet **Headplane**, a web interface for managing the popular self-hosted *Tailscale* alternative [*Headscale*](https://headscale.net/?ref=selfh.st). Developed to fill the gap left by Headscale's CLI-only config, **Headplane** provides an intuitive dashboard for managing subnets, machines, users, ACLs, DNS, and settings. Other features include remote web-based SSH for configured machines, SSO via OIDC, and detailed network information.

**Headplane** can be easily deployed via [bare metal](https://headplane.net/install/native-mode?ref=selfh.st) or [Docker](https://headplane.net/install/docker?ref=selfh.st) and requires a running instance of *Headscale*.
