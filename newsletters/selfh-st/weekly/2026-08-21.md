## Weekly Highlights

Every so often, I poke my head out of my self-hosting hole to (1) appreciate the chaos I avoid by self-hosting, and (2) watch the world burn. This week was one of those weeks.

AI watermarking dominated the conversation for much of it ([here's a neat primer](https://declaude.org/watermarking/?ref=selfh.st) if you need it). [Anthropic was forced to defend Claude's latest capabilities](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark?ref=selfh.st) given the [EU AI Act's new disclosure requirements](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/?ref=selfh.st), while later that same day, Google announced [Gemini users are now able to opt out of visible watermarks](https://x.com/GeminiApp/status/2088277477672255830?ref=selfh.st) on their "creations".

We also learned [RAM prices have climbed 500% in the last 12 months](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/ram/memory-prices-climb-500-percent-in-12-months-up-to-10x-the-lowest-ever-tracked-prices-128gb-of-ddr5-now-usd3-399?ref=selfh.st) from a recent study published by Tom's Hardware – just a few days before Stripe announced [they're buying OpenRouter for $7.5B](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/19/business/stripe-openrouter-ai.html?ref=selfh.st). (I wonder if there's a correlation...)

And if all of that sounds batsh*t to you, it gets worse:

- A [Kentucky school](https://gizmodo.com/which-ai-chatbot-did-this-school-use-to-make-such-a-ridiculous-map-2000800045?ref=selfh.st) shared a [bizarre AI-generated map of the U.S.](https://gizmodo.com/app/uploads/2026/08/google-nano-banana-image-generator.jpg?ref=selfh.st) with its students (I'm from *Vestrntane*, if you've ever wondered)
- Some guy [hid a prompt injection in his legal filing](https://www.404media.co/person-hides-prompt-injection-in-legal-filing-telling-ai-to-side-with-them/?ref=selfh.st) that encouraged the court's AI to rule in his favor (fortunately, the court confirmed it does not use AI for these reviews)
- The BBC uncovered a controversial practice called [destructive scanning](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cp3rprx2wl4o?ref=selfh.st), where AI companies are buying second-hand books and destroying their spines to make them easier to scan (sounds like they should have consulted with [this developer](https://www.reddit.com/r/raspberry_pi/comments/1voyops/i_built_a_raspberry_pi_book_scanner_that_turns/?ref=selfh.st))
- [Meta launched Pocket](https://www.neowin.net/news/meta-launches-new-vibe-coding-platform-pocket-in-the-us/?ref=selfh.st), a TikTok-style app for vibe coding mini-games and sharing them with others
- A keen-eyed developer discovered Apple's AirPods [may have cameras in them](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/17/camera-equipped-airpods-macos-26-7/?ref=selfh.st) based on a video found in the latest macOS release candidate (move over, [smart glasses](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/ng-interactive/2026/aug/19/meta-glasses-privacy-surveillance?ref=selfh.st))
- Mark Zuckerberg [purchased a castle in Ireland](https://www.irishtimes.com/property/2026/08/20/meta-boss-mark-zuckerberg-buys-co-waterford-castle/?ref=selfh.st) (?)

We really do live in the strangest timeline.

(If you read this newsletter for news that's actually relevant to self-hosting – GitHub [experienced an 8-hour outage](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/the-august-17-outage-and-the-work-ahead/?ref=selfh.st), Debian is [preparing a vote on AI contributions](https://www.debian.org/vote/2026/vote_002?ref=selfh.st), Plex [finally admitted their recent design changes stink](https://forums.plex.tv/t/navigation-updates-coming-to-preview-users/941751?ref=selfh.st), there's a [new contender for the worst-named *arr app](https://github.com/leestow/Censorarr?ref=selfh.st), and a reader asked me to share an [ad-free self-hosting break-even calculator](https://techfuelhq.com/tools/selfhost-breakeven?ref=selfh.st) they recently published.)

Happy **selfh.st**/ing!

## Content Spotlight

Meet **Floppy**, a self-hosted media tracker and alternative to Trakt, Letterboxd, and TV Time. **Floppy** gives users a unified interface for tracking movies, TV, anime, books, games, music, and podcasts from a modern grid-based interface. Features include watch history, recap statistics, shareable lists, owned-media tracking, and sync integrations for automation (Plex, Jellyfin, Audiobookshelf, etc.).

**Floppy **can be [easily deployed via Docker](https://github.com/dannyvfilms/Floppy?ref=selfh.st#install) and requires a separate Redis (or [compatible service](https://selfh.st/apps/?tag=In-Memory%20Cache)) container.

## Command Line Corner

Run **docker system prune** every so often to remove dangling containers, volumes, and images:

```bash
$ docker system prune

  Total reclaimed space: 34.1GB
```
