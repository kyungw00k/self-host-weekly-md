## Overview

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/selfhst/cdn/assets/icons/sponsor/coder.png)

AI coding agents run on your laptop with your credentials, your network access, and no record of what they touched. Coder is open source and gives every agent a [**self-hosted workspace**](https://coder.com/docs/install?utm_campaign=26Q3MS_Self_Host_Weekly_Sponsorship_SEP&utm_source=selfhst&utm_medium=media-sponsorship&utm_content=page) you define in Terraform, so your code never leaves the machines you own.

## Weekly Highlights

The **Jellyfin** team took a page from [Microsoft's playbook](https://www.fastcompany.com/3036464/with-windows-10-microsoft-will-skip-a-version-number-and-go-back-to-basics?ref=selfh.st) and skipped a major version number (v11) with [the release of v12 this week](https://jellyfin.org/posts/jellyfin-release-12.0?ref=selfh.st) – just three months after its [v10.11 release](https://github.com/jellyfin/jellyfin/releases?ref=selfh.st#release-v10.11.11). (While the team provides the rationale behind the jump in the release notes, I can only assume it's because [the project's new leadership](https://forum.jellyfin.org/t-project-leadership-changes?ref=selfh.st) can't count.)

The major update comes packed with a number of new features (proper book support, multi-version episodes, improved recommendations), but the biggest upgrades come in the form of database refinements that users are claiming significantly decrease load times and a new "modern" layout.

Meanwhile in **Plex**-land, [over 36,000 servers have yet to upgrade](https://www.scworld.com/brief/over-36000-plex-media-servers-remain-unpatched-against-security-vulnerabilities?ref=selfh.st) to a [urgent security patch](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/plex-warns-users-to-patch-security-vulnerabilities-immediately/?ref=selfh.st) the company says addresses several critical vulnerabilities.

But don't worry – regardless of where you sit in the self-hosted streaming wars, LG is (allegedly) [always watching you](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/lg-tv-shown-capable-of-tracking-user-activity-even-when-offline/?ref=selfh.st)...

In other news and activity:

- **Discord**'s [delayed age verification requirements have returned](https://www.gadgetreview.com/discord-is-bringing-back-age-verification-for-millions-of-users?ref=selfh.st) (and I'm sure most of us will continue to not do anything about it)
- **Bitwarden**'s latest update allows users to run the password manager [rootless and on a read-only filesystem](https://github.com/bitwarden/self-host/releases/tag/v2026.8.1?ref=selfh.st)
- Matt Mullenweg, the CEO of **Automattic** and co-founder of **WordPress**, was [placed on a leave of absence](https://www.404media.co/wordpress-automattic-ceo-matt-mullenweg-put-on-leave-of-absence/?ref=selfh.st) (presumably for a number of incidents outlined in the linked article)
- ich777, a well known Unraid community developer, announced [they're moving on from Unraid](https://forums.unraid.net/topic/200476-moving-on-it-was-a-lot-of-fun-thanks-guys/?ref=selfh.st) (presumably to devote more time to [MOS](https://mos-official.net/?ref=selfh.st), their new NAS OS for homelabs)
- **Notifuse** (email newsletters) [updated the project's license to BSL-1.1](https://github.com/Notifuse/notifuse/blob/main/LICENSING.md?ref=selfh.st) with [an incredibly wordy and AI-generated explanation](https://github.com/Notifuse/notifuse/releases/tag/v40.0?ref=selfh.st) that makes me want to throw my laptop out the window
- **Papermerge** (document management) seems to be on the path to abandonment [after the dev announced they're shifting their focus to its cloud/SaaS version](https://github.com/papermerge/papermerge-core?ref=selfh.st#%EF%B8%8F-important-notice)
- **Figranium** (workflow automation) [took a jab at "open source" projects](https://figranium.dev/blog/figranium-pro-continued-commitment-open-source?ref=selfh.st) that actually operate under an open core licensing model
- [Daniel Brendel](https://github.com/danielbrendel?ref=selfh.st) – the developer behind [HortusFox](https://github.com/danielbrendel/hortusfox-web?ref=selfh.st) – released a [virtual pet game](https://github.com/danielbrendel/krepagotchi-game?ref=selfh.st) that's a cross between Minecraft and Tamagotchi

Happy **selfh.st**/ing!

## Content Spotlight

Meet **nextExplorer**, a self-hosted web-based file explorer. With **nextExplorer**, users can easily browse, view, edit, and manage files on a server directly from a browser. from anywhere. Features include multi-user support, SSO, quick previews, built-in file editors, link-based sharing, smart search, and flexible layout options/views.

**nextExplorer** can be [easily deployed via Docker](https://github.com/nxzai/NextExplorer?ref=selfh.st#quickstart-docker-compose) and doesn't require additional containers.
