## Weekly Highlights

I found myself gushing over the [Immich](https://immich.app/?ref=selfh.st) team several times this week, most notably for [their speedy response](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/tag/v3.3.6?ref=selfh.st) after [somebody discovered](https://old.reddit.com/r/selfhosted/comments/1xnbujt/psa_immich_machine_learning_has_microsoft/?ref=selfh.st) a dependency in their machine learning container recently enabled Microsoft telemetry data collection by default. (If you're a few versions behind, use `ORT_DISABLE_TELEMETRY=1`* *in your compose file to disable it.)

But the week's activity that had me actually etching *Ethan ♥ *[*Alex*](https://github.com/alextran1502?ref=selfh.st) onto a tree was a commit from the app's [v3.4.0 beta release](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases?ref=selfh.st#release-v3.4.0-rc.1) that [finally brings tags to mobile apps (!)](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/pull/30753?ref=selfh.st).

Tags were first released *back in August 2024* ([v1.113.0](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/tag/v1.113.0?ref=selfh.st)) as a flexible alternative to albums. Unfortunately, despite the changelog's warning that they were limited to the web, I didn't realize it until I'd already converted my entire collection – and I've been lowkey harassing the team about it ([1](https://selfh.st/weekly/2026-06-19/), [2](https://selfh.st/weekly/2026-07-31/)) ever since.

In other news, AI (and more specifically, *OpenAI*) found itself a bit of a laughing stock this week after Sam Altman made a remark about [how terrible it is for people to have RAM in their personal computers rather than data centers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iMsUcCYNKqY&t=1764s&pp=0gcJCTEAlc8ueATH&ref=selfh.st) (he's [since taken it back](https://x.com/sama/status/2117846298472886419?a=A2halI37G9cOuA1ktMA&s=20&ref=selfh.st), but there's no stopping the memes at this point).

And that's not all. Just a few days later, a spokesperson from the company indicated they've begun internally referring to data centers as [*compute campuses**](https://www.businessinsider.com/openai-ai-data-centers-rebrand-compute-campuses-midterms-2026-11?ref=selfh.st) *(does that make my homelab a *compute compound*?).

Other activity and happenings that you should be aware of:

- **Wiki.js** [v3.0](https://js.wiki/blog/2026-wiki-js-3-release/?ref=selfh.st), an update to the popular wiki platform that has been in the works [for what feels like forever](https://old.reddit.com/r/selfhosted/comments/1mebp67/is_wikijs_3_dead/?ref=selfh.st), was finally released
- **Home Assistant** officially renamed HACS to Marketplace in its November release and also teased a [product launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YpcrIy5f774&ref=selfh.st) set to be announced next Wednesday (11/11)
- **Samsung** reminded users they're [terminating free access to the SmartThings API](https://www.sammobile.com/news/smartthings-api-access-will-soon-require-a-5-monthly-payment/?ref=selfh.st), and thus [LocalThings](https://github.com/mbillow/localthings?ref=selfh.st) was born
- **Finamp**, a popular Jellyfin music streaming client, [officially launched v1.0.0](https://github.com/finamp-app/finamp/releases?ref=selfh.st#release-1.0.1) – the first stable release of its completely redesigned interface
- An **Obsidian **user grew tired of the influx of new vibe coded plugins – so they developed one of their own [that does nothing](https://github.com/sty-ilman/nothing?ref=selfh.st) (and it [isn't the first time](https://github.com/kelseyhightower/nocode?ref=selfh.st) we've seen something like this)
- A few weeks ago, Gamers Nexus [released a video outlining LG's sketchy privacy practices](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IFVTcM28KA&ref=selfh.st) with its **smart TV** line – and a [new study from Northeastern University](https://watchingthewatchers.github.io/watchingthewatchers/paper.html?ref=selfh.st) suggests the issue is more widespread than we realized
- Revolut, a British FinTech company, made headlines after [announcing they've cut AI development costs 10x](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/03/british-fintech-revolut-slashes-ai-coding-costs-10x-by-cutting-out-claude-and-going-self-hosted/5307345?ref=selfh.st) by shifting to **self-hosted models**
- I stumbled upon [hyperbin](https://github.com/hypernuclear/hyperbin?ref=selfh.st), a (vibe coded) desktop app that makes your recycle bin visually collect flies and debris as it fills up
- The internet tried to figure out [whether GitHub's awesome-lists are still awesome](https://erikgahner.github.io/posts/2026/awesome-repositories-are-no-longer-awesome/?ref=selfh.st)

Happy **selfh.st**/ing!

## Command Line Corner

Use the **-m** flag when calling **mkdir** to simultaneously set the permissions of a new directory when creating it:

```bash
$ mkdir -m 770 exampledirectory
$ ls -l
  drwxrwx--- user group exampledirectory
```
